About

Hi, I'm Katie. For 15 years I've built content ecosystems for B2B and SaaS brands — as an executive editor, a content director, and a community lead for a global tech audience. Most recently, I spearheaded The CTO Club from launch to $40K+ in weekly revenue, 1.9M unique site visitors in year one, and 38% average newsletter open rates as a solo shop, owning strategy, workflows, and execution across every channel.

I'm a true Xennial: nostalgic for analog life, all in on digital, and riding the AI wave without losing sight of the humans it's supposed to serve. I build structured, human-in-the-loop AI workflows that let lean teams produce more (without sacrificing editorial judgment, brand voice, or quality).

I'm based in Raleigh, NC, and work with clients remotely, on a fractional or project basis.

What we do

Struggling with bandwidth, senior judgment, or a workflow that hasn't caught up to AI? Here are a few ways we typically fill that gap — most engagements end up as a mix of these

Fractional Content & Editorial Leadership

Embedded, part-time leadership for teams that need an experienced editor-in-chief or content director without a full-time hire.

  • Set editorial standards and workflows your team can run without me
  • Manage and mentor existing writers or contributors
  • Own the content calendar and hit publishing deadlines
  • Step in for interim leadership gaps without a lengthy ramp-up

AI Content Systems & Workflows

Structured, human-in-the-loop AI workflows for research, drafting, optimization, and repurposing — built to scale output without losing brand voice.

  • Build repeatable AI-assisted research and drafting workflows
  • Train your team on where AI helps and where it hurts quality
  • Repurpose long-form content into multiple formats and channels
  • Keep brand voice consistent as output scales

Content Strategy & Audits

Full-funnel content strategy, competitive audits, and SEO/AEO/GEO optimization designed for human readers and AI-driven search.

  • Align and troubleshoot messaging that isn't landing
  • Define primary and secondary audiences, and where they diverge
  • Set short-term and long-term content roadmaps
  • Flag the milestones and roles that determine whether it works

Newsletter Creation, Support & Scaling

Newsletter strategy from zero to launch, or from stagnant to scaling — editorial calendars, growth tactics, and hands-on production support.

  • Launch a newsletter from scratch, including positioning and cadence
  • Diagnose why an existing newsletter has stalled
  • Build growth tactics for list size and open rates
  • Handle end-to-end production so it ships on schedule

Copyediting & Editorial QA

Line-level editing and quality standards that keep every piece of content publish-ready, consistent, and on-brand.

  • Catch inconsistencies in tone, style, and terminology before publish
  • Build a style guide your whole team can reference
  • Run a final QA pass so nothing ships with easy-to-miss errors
  • Keep every contributor's voice on-brand, not homogenized

Community Building & Support

Growing communities from the ground up: recruiting the right voices, keeping them engaged, and building the kind of culture that sustains itself.

  • Recruit and onboard the right early contributors or members
  • Design engagement rituals that keep people showing up
  • Identify who should own community moderation long-term
  • Build the culture and norms that outlast any single manager
